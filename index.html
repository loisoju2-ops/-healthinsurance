National Health Insurance Authorization

Nigerians living in the Diaspora have an opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the lives of their families back home by helping them obtain health insurance.

Supporting health insurance for parents, children, siblings, and extended family members is more than sending money for medical bills. It is an investment in family protection, dignity, financial security, and longer life.

Our purpose is to connect Nigerians living abroad with affordable health insurance coverage for their families in their home countries.
